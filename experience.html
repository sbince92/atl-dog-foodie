03 — Good to know

Questions we get every week.

How much does boarding cost?

$25 a day, or $150 a week for longer stays. The weekly rate kicks in automatically once a stay reaches seven days.

How and when do I pay?

Full payment is due up front once your stay is confirmed. If you extend while your dog is with us, payment for the extra days is due within 24 hours. We take Cash App, Zelle, Square and cash.

Are there breed or size restrictions?

No. Every breed and size is welcome. The only dogs we can't take are aggressive ones, for the safety of the other guests.

What about fleas, ticks and heartworm?

We ask for proof your dog is on flea medication. If they aren't, we'll give a monthly NexGard Plus flea-and-heartworm pill at drop-off for $30.

What should I pack?

Their regular food portioned per meal, any medication with instructions, and a blanket or toy that smells like home. If you'd rather not bring food, we'll provide it: $5 a day for dogs up to 40 lb, $10 a day over 40 lb.

Can I add a bath or nail trim?

Yes. A bath with flea-and-tick medicated shampoo and conditioner is $25, and a nail trim is $7. Tick them on the booking form or ask at drop-off.

Do you offer supplements?

Yes. We can give a daily vitamin for shedding, mood, joints and general health for $3 a day. Add it on the booking form.
